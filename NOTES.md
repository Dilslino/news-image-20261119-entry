## Design

Front-page modern (concept C): masthead "THE WORLD TODAY" + date line
"THURSDAY 19 NOVEMBER 2026", a large photo-style hero of the Shenzhen
summit handshake with the red GTA VI box beside it, and a dense 4-column
grid of 30 further headlines — 32 stories in total. Chosen over 6
candidates from 3 concepts:

- (a) classic broadsheet, pure type (2 variants) — very dense and clean,
  but the small sub-lines invented numbers/prices/scores, and one variant
  had garbled filler lines ("YEOWDIKE DIS NOVEMSHEER").
- (b) mosaic card grid (30 cards with icons) — flawless text, no invented
  details, but reads as a poster, not a newspaper; one card's icon bled a
  stray "M" onto the Alaska map.
- (c) modern front page, hero + grid (2 variants) — WINNER. The GTA-neon
  variant cropped its bottom grid row; the summit-hero variant is fully
  legible, has no invented sub-line details (headline-only grid), and the
  strongest visual hierarchy. Its hero is an illustration of the day's
  headline event, the trilateral meeting scheduled for 19 November.

Lesson applied from NI18 V1 (94.7594%, rank 7295/8260): a 7-story
timeline loses on density to front pages with 30–44 stories. This entry
is dense (32 stories) AND legible — every headline spelled correctly,
no garbled text.
